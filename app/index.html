The road to Vice Got a Price!

Six phases, in the order a first playthrough should meet them. The story comes first — the game has no mission replay, and four trophies can be lost on the way — then the long free-roam sweep, then the 100 percent checklist that feeds the platinum. Every trophy appears in exactly one phase; tap any chip to jump to its guide.
