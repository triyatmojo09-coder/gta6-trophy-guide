Roadmap to the platinum

Six phases, built from the official descriptions: story first with the missables watched, strands closed before the endgame, then the collection, activity and crime sweeps, and 100% last. Nothing outside Phase 1 is missable — every other trophy can be earned in post-story free roam, so the only real deadline is your own patience.

Sources & method
